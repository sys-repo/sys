### R3-A01 — accept P2: nested origin arrays execute before policy capture completes

Source: `code/sys/server/src/m.server.dist/u.materialize/u.input.ts`, especially `snapshotPolicy`,
`snapshotResponsePolicy` and `snapshotOrigins`.

The live code snapshots manifest/resources before verification limits and iterates origin arrays
with `for...of`. Exact record checks do not extend to array elements or the iterator. An element
getter or custom iterator can mutate still-borrowed verification/resource authority before it is
copied. The outer catch cannot undo executed hooks and also conflates exceptional nested policy
rejection with invalid top-level input. The report's direct-materializer counterexample follows this
control flow.

Retained defect, independently visible in the HEAD-side diff. Generation's inspected
`u.generation/u.input.ts` preflights nested data and rejects the proposed decorated arrays; do not
claim that this reproduces a Generation bypass or remote JSON attack.

Smallest consequence: admit origin arrays as exact dense own data at the materialization input
owner, without iterator/accessor/Proxy execution. Preserve input-versus-policy precedence and
legitimate lifecycle semantics. Reordering verification alone is insufficient: other uncaptured
policy fields would remain mutable through these hooks.

Closing proof: inert-array success; accessor, iterator, Proxy and revoked-array refusal with no
hook, credential, filesystem or network execution; original tight budgets cannot be widened;
combined invalid lifecycle/policy preserves precedence. Counterexamples have not yet been executed.
